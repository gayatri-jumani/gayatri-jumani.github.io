FAQ

Common questions

Which grades and subjects do you teach?

Grades 1 to 8 in Mathematics, Science, Social Science (History, Geography, Civics), English and Hindi.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The 15-minute call is a chance for us to talk about your child's needs. There is no obligation.

Do you teach advanced chess players?

No. My chess classes are only for beginners: children (and adults) who have never played or know just the basics.

Which boards do you teach?

All boards: CBSE, ICSE and State board.

What are the fees?

School students (Grades 1 to 8): Rs 500 per hour. Chess for beginners: Rs 600 per hour.

What are the class timings?

It depends on the schedule. Classes are usually 1 hour long.

Are classes online or in person?

Both. Online classes are one-to-one (individual). In-person classes can be in a group or individual, depending on the schedule. In-person classes are in Kilpauk, Chennai.
