Chess

Chess for Beginners ♟️

Gentle, fun chess lessons for people who have never played, or know only the basics.

Beginners only. These classes are not designed for advanced or tournament players.
Book a free 15-min call

Who it's for

  • Children or adults who have never played chess
  • Those who know only the basics (for example, how a few pieces move)
  • Anyone who wants to learn in a relaxed, no-pressure way

Not for: advanced players looking for high-level coaching, tournament training or engine analysis.

What is taught

  • Setting up the board
  • How each piece moves
  • Basic rules (capturing, special moves)
  • Check and checkmate
  • Simple openings
  • Simple tactics
  • Playing real games and learning from them
Why chess

Benefits for your child

🎯

Focus

Chess trains children to concentrate on one thing for a stretch of time.

🌱

Patience

Good moves take thought. Children learn to slow down and not rush.

🧭

Planning

Thinking a few steps ahead is a skill that helps in studies and in life.

Format

How classes work

Lessons start with the basics and move ahead one small step at a time, mixing short explanations with plenty of playing.
