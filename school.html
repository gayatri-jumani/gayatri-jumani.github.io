What is covered

By grade band

Note: This is a high-level guide. I follow your child's school syllabus and board, and adjust to their pace. [ADD: boards you teach, e.g. CBSE / ICSE / State]

Grades 1 to 3

Building strong foundations

Mathematics

Numbers, counting, addition and subtraction, early multiplication, shapes, measurement.

Science

Plants, animals, our body, food, water, simple observation.

Social Science

Family, community, places around us, simple maps, good habits.

English

Phonics, reading, simple sentences, vocabulary, early writing.

Hindi

Varnamala, matras, reading simple words and sentences, early writing.

Grades 4 to 5

Growing independence

Mathematics

Place value, multiplication and division, fractions, decimals, area and perimeter, word problems.

Science

Living things, materials, force and energy, the environment, simple experiments.

Social Science

Our country, states, maps, early history, how local government works.

English

Reading comprehension, grammar basics, paragraph and letter writing.

Hindi

Reading comprehension, grammar basics, paragraph and letter writing.

Grades 6 to 7

Deeper concepts

Mathematics

Integers, fractions and decimals, ratio, basic algebra, geometry, data handling.

Science

Matter, motion, light, heat, the human body, plants and life processes.

Social Science

History timelines, geography of India and the world, civics and government.

English

Grammar, essays and letters, reading comprehension, literature.

Hindi

Grammar, essays and letters, comprehension, literature.

Grades 8 to 9

Exam-ready and confident

Mathematics

Algebra, mensuration, geometry, statistics, building towards board-level maths.

Science

Physics, Chemistry and Biology concepts, numericals and diagrams.

Social Science

History, Geography, Civics and Economics with answer-writing practice.

English

Advanced grammar, writing skills, literature, comprehension.

Hindi

Grammar, writing skills, literature, comprehension.
