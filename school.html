What is covered

By grade band

Note: This is a high-level guide to typical topics, in line with the NCERT (CBSE) syllabus and the ICSE and State board syllabi. Exact chapters follow your child's school and textbooks, and I adjust to their pace. I teach all boards: CBSE, ICSE and State. Under ICSE, Science and Social Science are often studied as separate subjects (Physics, Chemistry, Biology; History and Civics, Geography), and I cover these too.

Grades 1 to 3

Building strong foundations

Mathematics

Numbers up to 100, then 1000, addition and subtraction, introduction to multiplication, shapes and patterns, measurement, time and money.

Science

Environmental Studies (EVS): plants, animals, our body, food, water, family, houses and good habits.

Social Science

Taught through EVS: family, neighbourhood, community helpers, our surroundings and simple maps.

English

Phonics, reading, simple sentences, poems and stories, basic grammar and vocabulary, early writing.

Hindi

Varnamala, matras, reading words and sentences, poems and stories, simple writing.

Grades 4 to 5

Growing independence

Mathematics

Large numbers, the four operations, factors and multiples, fractions, decimals, money, time, perimeter and area, angles, patterns and data handling.

Science

Environmental Studies (EVS): plants and animals, food and health, water, shelter, travel and transport, weather and our environment.

Social Science

Taught through EVS: people and places, maps, and how communities live and work.

English

Reading comprehension, grammar (nouns, verbs, adjectives, tenses), vocabulary, paragraph and letter writing.

Hindi

Reading comprehension, grammar (sangya, sarvanam, visheshan, kriya), paragraph and letter writing.

Grades 6 to 7

Deeper concepts

Mathematics

Whole numbers and integers, factors, HCF and LCM, fractions and decimals, ratio and proportion, introduction to algebra and simple equations, basic geometry, perimeter and area, data handling.

Science

Food and nutrition, fibre to fabric, materials, motion, light, heat, electricity and circuits, magnets, acids and bases, plants, the human body, air, water and weather.

Social Science

History (early and medieval India), Geography (the Earth, climate, resources, natural vegetation), Civics (diversity, government, democracy).

English

Grammar, comprehension, letter and essay writing, prose and poetry from the textbook.

Hindi

Grammar, comprehension, letter and essay writing, prose and poetry from the textbook.

Grade 8

Exam-ready and confident

Mathematics

Rational numbers, linear equations in one variable, quadrilaterals, squares and cubes with roots, algebraic expressions and identities, factorisation, mensuration, exponents and powers, direct and inverse proportion, data handling and graphs.

Science

Crop production, microorganisms, metals and non-metals, coal and petroleum, the cell, reproduction, force and pressure, friction, sound, light, electric current, stars and the solar system.

Social Science

History (colonial India and the freedom struggle), Geography (resources, agriculture, industries), Civics (the Indian Constitution, parliament, judiciary and laws).

English

Grammar, comprehension, formal and informal writing, prose and poetry from the textbook.

Hindi

Grammar, comprehension, formal and informal writing, prose and poetry from the textbook.
